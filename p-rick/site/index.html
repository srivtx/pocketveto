P-001WORKING PAPER

The Personal Event Bus: User-Owned Middleware for Ambient Digital-Life Events

No shipping product, standard, or research system combines ambient capture, durable append-only storage, schema normalization, typed third-party subscriptions, and user-held ownership.

We name and specify the missing layer of personal computing: an append-only, schema-normalized local event log with a permission calculus, positioned against fifteen adjacent systems (Solid, IFTTT, Home Assistant, MyLifeBits, Databox…), with adoption mechanics grounded in a shipping case study and a regulatory argument for event-level data portability. 12 pages.

P-002WORKING PAPER

Consumer Rights as Dead Capital: The Case for a Personal Entitlement Engine

Rights are issued without an execution layer — €3–6B/yr unclaimed flight compensation, class-action claim rates ≤10%, $3B/yr forfeited gift-card value.

Borrowing de Soto’s dead capital and the administrative-burden literature, we formalize the claim-execution decision, specify the entitlement engine (event ingestion × rights corpus × deadline ledger × delegated filing with an escalation ladder), and read five failed or partial predecessors as cumulative design constraints rather than refutations. 13 pages.

P-003WORKING PAPER

The n=1 Cost-of-Living Index: Statistically Defensible Personal Inflation from Local-First Purchase Streams

No consumer software computes a personal price index that would survive statistical review — the 2025–26 cohort ships sentiment machinery.

We formalize the n=1 estimation problem (unit-value bias, sparse matching, chain drift, shrinkflation, small-sample uncertainty) and give a construction that survives it: two-level item canonization, matched-model elementary indices, drift control, explicit quality adjustment, Bayesian shrinkage to official priors, and interval publication rules that refuse to overclaim. 12 pages.

About p-rick

p-rick is an independent research program with one method: survey the software landscape for genuine gaps (not toys, not wrappers, not AI-era redundancies), verify the gap survives adversarial review, then write the paper that names the category and specifies the missing system. Every paper is paired with an essay, because research that nobody reads might as well not exist.

The program lives in the same repository as its first product substrate, PocketVeto — an on-device autopay tracker whose notification-capture stack is the existence proof for P-001. Work is tracked automatically in p-rick/agents.md.

Papers are working papers (v1.0), CC BY 4.0. Figures marked as industry- or regulator-sourced carry their conflicts of interest; our relationship with PocketVeto is disclosed in each paper.
